systems philosophy
//
Reliability over hype
A system that works predictably is worth more than one that's technically impressive but fragile under real conditions.
//
Measure before optimizing
Benchmarks first, changes second. Guessing at bottlenecks wastes time; structured logging and profiling find them.
//
Async where latency is unpredictable
When a dependency can take 100ms or 8s, decouple the request lifecycle from processing. Synchronous design breaks at the tail.
//
Visibility before scaling
You can't scale what you can't observe. Instrumentation and structured logs belong in v1, not as a later addition when things go wrong.
